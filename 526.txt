OUTFIT: This grunge-inspired sleepwear look features an oversized faded black T-shirt with the instantly recognizable Nirvana yellow smiley-face logo printed large across the chest, deliberately distressed and slightly cracked like a vintage band tee. The heavyweight cotton has dropped shoulders, broad sleeves, a relaxed crew neck, and an extra-long hem falling toward the thighs. It is worn completely loose and untucked. Thick charcoal socks with yellow accents and oversized black fuzzy slippers complete the relaxed rock-night aesthetic.
