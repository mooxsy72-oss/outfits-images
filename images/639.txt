The outfit features a high-contrast minimalist look defined by an oversized light blue shirt paired with tight black shorts and matching black accessories. The solid light blue button-down shirt is constructed from a crisp, mid-weight cotton fabric that holds structured volume. It features a wide, unbuttoned pointed collar, dramatically dropped shoulders, and loose, long sleeves ending in elongated, stiff cuffs. The body of the shirt is tightly wrapped and tucked at the waist, creating full, billowy volume on top while keeping the waist compressed. The solid black tailored shorts fit closely to the hips, featuring a high-rise waist with belt loops and an ultra-short length that exposes the entire length of the legs. A thin, smooth black leather belt with a polished silver-tone rectangular buckle cinches the waist. On the feet are glossy black patent leather slingback pumps with a sharp pointed toe and a thin stiletto heel. A structured, rectangular black leather handbag with a rigid top handle and a prominent front flap finishes the look.
