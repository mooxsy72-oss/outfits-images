This streetwear ensemble features a light-wash denim color palette contrasted by a solid white base layer. The top layer consists of a tightly cropped denim shrug jacket with long sleeves, a pointed collar, and breast pockets, worn open over a form-fitting, sleeveless white ribbed scoop-neck tank top that exposes the lower midriff. The low-rise, light-wash denim jeans feature an exaggerated, oversized, wide-leg silhouette that pools over the footwear. A large, stylized gothic text embroidery runs vertically down the left pant leg, and a prominent rectangular patch is stitched near the front waistband. Bulky silver athletic sneakers ground the look.
