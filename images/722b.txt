This minimalist outfit features a clean cream and beige color palette without any handheld accessories. The upper layer consists of a slouchy, cropped beige knit sweater crafted from a soft, medium-weight melange yarn, featuring a ribbed mock neck and relaxed balloon sleeves with elongated ribbed cuffs. The lower body is defined by high-waisted, secondary cream wide-leg denim jeans that provide a heavy, structured drape that pools heavily over the floor. All heavy winter outerwear and heeled boots are completely removed, leaving the feet entirely bare beneath the denim hems.
