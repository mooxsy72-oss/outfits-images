This feminine, casual outfit centers on a white mini dress paired with a cropped light-blue denim jacket. The dress features a highly structured, corset-style bodice with distinct vertical boning lines and a straight neckline held up by thin camisole straps, exposing the collarbones and shoulders. Its waist transitions into an extremely voluminous, flared A-line skirt with dense, structured pleating that holds its shape away from the body, ending mid-thigh. Layered over it is a rigid, light-wash denim jacket featuring a distinct cropped hemline, standard collar, buttoned chest pockets, and long sleeves. On the feet, low-top leather sneakers repeat the palette with alternating panels of crisp white and soft pastel blue. The entire ensemble follows a clean, two-tone color scheme with no patterns.
