This modern two-piece ensemble consists of a structured tweed romper layered over a long-sleeved blouse, creating an overall black-and-white color palette dominated by dark tones with white accents. The black-and-white woven tweed fabric features a textured plaid-like pattern. The romper features a plunging V-neckline, a fitted waist, and structured shorts with a short length that exposes the thighs and legs. It is trimmed with a contrasting white ruffle at the bottom hem and white faux-pocket flaps at the hips. Layered underneath, the lightweight white blouse has a high neckline and voluminous, long bishop sleeves gathered into wide cuffs. The outfit is paired with a small black leather handbag and clear PVC heeled sandals that leave the feet entirely visible.
