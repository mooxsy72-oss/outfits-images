This casual winter outfit features a balanced black and cream color palette with a striped pattern. The outermost layer is a bulky, oversized black leather shearling jacket with a wide cream faux-fur lapel collar, matching fur cuffs, and a straight hem. Underneath sits a slightly cropped cream ribbed-knit half-zip sweater featuring horizontal black sailor stripes, a high collar, and long sleeves with solid cream cuffs. The lower body is defined by high-waisted, secondary cream wide-leg denim jeans that provide a structured drape down to the floor. Accessories include black fluffy faux-fur earmuffs, a long black knit scarf with fringed edges, and black leather ankle boots with chunky block heels and squared-off pointed toes.
