This minimalist, casual athleisure outfit utilizes a high-contrast black and light gray color scheme without patterns. The base layer features a tight black crew-neck top. The lower half is a solid black, high-waisted column maxi skirt made of stretchy jersey knit that hugs the hips and falls straight to the ankles. Layered above is a loose, oversized heather gray fleece sweatshirt, cropped aggressively to sit just beneath the chest. It features an open half-zip polo collar, wide dropped shoulders, and long slouchy sleeves that gather with ribbed cuffs. On the feet are clean white leather sneakers with flat soles, matching white laces, and a small black contrasting geometric motif on the sides. Accessories include a thin silver pendant chain necklace, small gold hoop earrings, and a small, structured black nylon shoulder bag with a curved top and thin black strap.
