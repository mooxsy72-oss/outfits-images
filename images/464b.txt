OUTFIT: This avant-garde streetwear ensemble features an eclectic, multi-layered color blocking scheme dominated by matte black and heather gray. The primary layer consists of a tight, secondary-skin top in thin, high-stretch jersey, tucked into very short, structured heather-gray wool shorts with a curved, high-rise waistband. On the legs are opaque black tights providing full coverage under the garments. The feet are fully covered by the smooth, unbroken opaque black tights, with no shoes or additional socks worn.
