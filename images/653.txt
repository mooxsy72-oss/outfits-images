This modern two-piece skirt suit is dominated by a slate gray color, crafted from a smooth, mid-weight suiting fabric with strong shape retention. The structured jacket features a collarless, deep V-neckline that exposes a white, straight-neck top underneath. It is tightly tailored at the waist with four self-covered buttons, creating a sharp hourglass silhouette enhanced by padded, defined shoulders and long, straight sleeves. The matching gray maxi skirt is high-waisted and skin-tight through the hips and thighs, draping down to the ankles with a front hem slit that exposes black pointed-toe stiletto pumps. A small, crescent-shaped shoulder bag in a tan and brown monogram canvas pattern serves as a prominent accessory.
