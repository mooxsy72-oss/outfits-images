The outfit centers on a long, oversized double-breasted overcoat in a thick, heavyweight matte wool blend fabric of a solid tobacco-brown color. It features prominent padded structured shoulders, exaggerated wide notched lapels, long straight sleeves, a deep chest pocket, and a multi-button closure creating a highly voluminous, enveloping silhouette that hangs past the knees. Layered beneath is a smooth, fine-gauge black knit turtleneck sweater providing complete upper body and neck coverage. This is tucked into high-waisted, straight-leg tailored black trousers in a heavy wool blend or structured canvas, which offer a clean, formal silhouette without being too tight or overly voluminous. A slim black leather belt with a polished square silver buckle cinches the waist. The color palette is starkly divided into a dominant black base with a secondary tobacco-brown top layer. On the feet, polished black leather boots anchor the refined, balanced proportions of the lower half.
