This western-inspired casual outfit focuses on a relaxed, indoor look. The one-piece medium-wash denim halter romper features a highly fitted, structured bodice with vertical panel seams, a center-front button placket, and two small faux-flap pockets at the hip line. The scoop neckline is trimmed with narrow white lace, extending into a halter strap that exposes the shoulders and arms. The lower section consists of tight-fitting, ultra-short shorts that expose the thighs and legs. The dominant material is mid-weight blue denim with stretch retention. The tall leather boots are completely removed, leaving the feet entirely bare. A single thin metallic bracelet is worn on the right wrist.
