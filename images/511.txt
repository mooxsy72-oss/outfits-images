OUTFIT: This contemporary gym-to-lounge ensemble uses deep graphite stretch jersey with an ultra-soft brushed surface. The dominant garment is a fitted cropped long-sleeved bolero jacket with a front zipper, narrow collar, close sleeves, and an extremely short hem that leaves the waist visible. Beneath it is a matching fitted square-neck sports bra. High-waisted full-length leggings tightly contour the waist, hips, thighs, and calves before ending in a clean straight hem. White retro-inspired sneakers create contrast. The monochromatic palette and sculpted proportions create a refined modern athleisure aesthetic.
