This cream-colored two-piece set features a solid, unpatterned color distribution utilizing a cropped bralette top and a draped maxi skirt. The fitted bralette top is constructed from a structured, mid-weight fabric featuring molded sweetheart cups, a gathered twist-front detail, and ultra-thin spaghetti straps that expose the shoulders and collarbones. The floor-length maxi skirt is made of a lightweight, fluidly draped jersey fabric with minimal shape retention. It features a high-waisted design gathered on the left hip through a prominent metallic silver ring, creating an extreme thigh-high slit and cascading loose folds that add visual volume to the silhouette. Metallic gold strappy sandals with thin cords across the foot anchor the ensemble. A gold wrist watch completes the look.
