This cream-colored mini wrap dress features a dominant large-scale orange and green floral pattern distributed evenly across the entire garment. The lightweight, fluid woven fabric provides soft drape and minimal stiffness, fitting tightly over the torso before flaring into a ruffled asymmetric hemline that exposes the upper thighs. The dress is constructed with a low scoop neckline, gathered cup detailing, and ultra-thin spaghetti straps that fully expose the shoulders, collarbones, and arms. A matching self-tie fabric wrap closure defines the waistline on the left side. Underneath the flared hem, a delicate cream lace trim peeks through, adding texture to the layered edge. The footwear is completely removed, leaving the feet entirely bare.
