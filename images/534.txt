OUTFIT: This warm cottage-inspired home ensemble features a loose sage-green waffle-knit henley top with a textured square weave, wooden buttons, dropped shoulders, and slightly rolled sleeves. The lower body consists of high-waisted cream cotton trousers with a relaxed tapered cut, elastic cuffs, and a soft brushed interior. A faded brown knitted cardigan is loosely draped over the shoulders, adding another layer without making the outfit bulky. Thick oatmeal socks and simple brown suede house slippers complete the look. The natural fabrics and muted earthy colors create a calm, comfortable silhouette with a subtle rustic character.
