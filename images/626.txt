This Y2K-inspired all-denim outfit consists of a cropped halter top, low-rise jeans, a quilted bag, and heels. The top is a highly structured, mid-weight blue denim halter with a plunging V-neckline, pointed handkerchief hem, and a central gold zip. It exposes the shoulders, arms, and full midriff. The low-rise, slim-fit jeans feature a subtle flare at the hem, classic five-pocket construction, and light fading down the thighs. A large, quilted blue denim shoulder bag with a gold chain strap dominates the left side. Simple pointy-toe black patent leather heels, a delicate gold pendant necklace, and thin gold bracelets complete the look.
