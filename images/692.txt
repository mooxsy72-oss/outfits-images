The outfit features a distinct autumn-inspired color palette dominated by terracotta red and warm earthy brown, with cream as a balancing neutral. The silhouette focuses on a sleek, high-waisted look where the top layer is cleanly tucked in to accentuate a very close, fitted body line from the torso through the legs.The upper layer consists of a terracotta red cardigan sweater crafted from a mid-weight jacquard knit. It features a deep V-neckline, long sleeves with tight ribbed cuffs, and large, sparse cream floral motifs with pink accents. This cardigan is worn closed and smoothly, very neatly tucked into the trousers to minimize bulk at the waist. Layered underneath is a thin, form-fitting cream turtleneck sweater with a subtle vertical cable-knit texture.The lower half features high-waisted, very tight, slim-fit earthy brown pants or skinny jeans. They are tailored from a structured, form-fitting stretch fabric with sharp vertical pintuck seams running down the center of each leg. The outfit is completed with a pair of elegant, sleek dark brown leather ankle boots with a tapered toe and a slim heel, color-matched to the trousers. A layered gold chain necklace with a circular carnelian stone pendant serves as the sole accessory.
