This minimalist lounge ensemble focuses entirely on a soft cream and beige color palette. The silhouette is defined by comfortable, slouchy proportions and soft textures. The upper body features a thick cream knit turtleneck sweater with vertical ribbed textures and a high, folded collar. It has relaxed long sleeves that drape over the wrists. This is tucked into high-waisted, voluminous cream sweatpants with a drawstring waistband. The trousers feature heavy, gathered fabric that creates fullness through the legs before tapering tightly into ribbed ankles. Thick-soled, ankle-height chestnut suede shearling boots add a single contrasting element of secondary brown color and anchor the relaxed, high-volume lower silhouette.
