This bold modern outfit pairs a cream cropped jacket-shirt with tailored high-waisted black trousers and sleek black high heels. The jacket features structured, padded shoulders and long tailored sleeves, but is cropped drastically under the bust with rounded hem panels, entirely exposing the midriff. Long cream fabric straps wrap criss-cross around the bare waist, tying in a bow at the front. It has a sharp, structured pointed collar and stays completely open down the center chest. The high-waisted trousers are made from a structured, mid-weight black suiting fabric with sharp front pleats, cinched by a thick black leather belt with a silver square buckle. The visual palette relies on solid block contrast between cream and black without patterns.
