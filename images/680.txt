This sleek evening look is composed of a loose-fitting black silk blouse, a white crop top, a high-waisted black leather midi skirt, and elegant black high heels.The long-sleeve blouse is made of lightweight, fluid black silk that drapes naturally, featuring a standard pointed collar and a deeply unbuttoned neckline. This pencil-style skirt features a clean, high waistband anchored by a slim black leather belt with a dark rectangular buckle, and it is defined by a bold, dramatic front thigh-high slit that exposes the left leg. The silhouette is lengthened by classic black leather stiletto high heels. The color scheme is predominantly solid black with a sharp accent of solid white, showing no patterns.
