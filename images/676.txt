This casual street-style outfit pairs a cream halter-neck crop top with exaggerated wide-leg blue jeans. The fitted top features a deep plunge neckline with a sharp polo-style collar, thin vertical ribs, a ruched center seam secured by three small buttons, and a split asymmetrical hem that exposes the midriff, shoulders, and arms. The high-waisted, rigid denim jeans dominate the silhouette with an intentionally oversized, balloon-like volume that puddles over the footwear, showing faint whiskering patterns near the pockets. Low-top skate sneakers complete the look, featuring beige suede uppers, chunky white laces, white leather side stripes, and a flat gum sole. The outfit maintains a solid color-blocked aesthetic of light cream and classic light-blue denim.
