This winter lounge ensemble features a dominant palette of crisp white and cream, contrasted by a secondary skin-toned beige base. The look balances extreme upper and lower volume against a form-fitting midsection. An oversized, cropped faux-fur jacket in pure white features horizontal quilted bands and voluminous full-length sleeves, worn with the large, deep hood resting down across the shoulders. Beneath, a skin-tight beige bodysuit covers the torso and legs, acting as a fitted base layer that contrasts with the bulky outerwear. Thick-soled, platform white snow boots anchor the look, detailed with criss-cross laces and a plush white faux-fur trim along the top collar.
