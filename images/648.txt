The outfit features a tailored, monochrome aesthetic dominated entirely by off-white tones. The visually dominant blazer dress is constructed from a heavy, structured woven fabric with strong shape retention and sharp tailoring. It features an ultra-fitted silhouette with padded shoulders, a deep overlapping wrap-style front, and a sharp notched lapel forming a plunging V-neckline that fully exposes the upper chest and collarbones. Its full-length sleeves are tight and taper cleanly down to the wrists. Cinching tightly at the natural waist, the dress fastens with four polished gold-tone buttons before transitioning into an ultra-short skirt that completely exposes the legs, detailed with a small left-thigh slit and an elongated trailing panel at the back hem. On the feet are matching off-white patent leather pumps featuring a sharp pointed toe and a slender stiletto heel. A delicate gold pendant necklace subtly accents the open neckline.
