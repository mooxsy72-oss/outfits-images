This streetwear ensemble features a minimalist denim and white color palette. The upper body outerwear is removed, exposing a form-fitting, sleeveless white ribbed scoop-neck tank top that completely leaves the arms, shoulders, and lower midriff bare. This is paired with low-rise, light-wash denim jeans featuring an exaggerated, oversized, wide-leg silhouette that pools over the footwear. A large, stylized gothic text embroidery runs vertically down the left pant leg, and a prominent rectangular patch is stitched near the front waistband. Bulky silver athletic sneakers ground the look.
