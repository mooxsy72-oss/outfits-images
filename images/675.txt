This cozy, monochromatic outfit features an oversized beige ribbed sweater paired with a cream-colored denim mini skirt. The chunky knit sweater has a wide, overlapping criss-cross V-neckline designed to sit off the shoulders, exposing the collarbones. Its silhouette is intentionally voluminous with long, slouchy balloon sleeves that bunch heavily at the ribbed cuffs, draping over the hands. The sweater is loosely tucked into a structured, high-waisted denim mini skirt that fits closely against the hips and ends at the upper thighs, featuring a standard zipper-and-button fly. On the feet, chunky platform sneakers continue the tone-on-tone theme with mixed panels of beige suede and mesh fabric. The wearable accessories include a delicate gold chain necklace with a small heart-shaped pendant and classic medium-sized gold hoop earrings.
