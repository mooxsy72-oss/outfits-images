The outfit features a severely cropped, oversized aviator shearling jacket in a solid matte cream-beige shade, offering structured visual volume on top. It is constructed from thick, shape-retaining faux suede with a plush tonal faux fur lining that forms a wide spread collar, exposed raw-edge seams, a silver metal zipper placket, and a small buckle throat latch. This top layer is tucked into high-waisted, slim-fit skinny jeans made of rigid, light-wash blue denim with a classic metal button fly. The color palette is split between a dominant cream-beige top and a secondary light-blue denim bottom. To anchor this top-heavy silhouette, add a pair of chunky cream-colored leather chelsea boots with a thick lug sole.
