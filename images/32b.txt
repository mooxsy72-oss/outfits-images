OUTFIT: A cohesive, academic-inspired ensemble features a dark green and burgundy palette, drawing influence from modern preppy, academy, and Japanese subculture aesthetics. Cropped Jacket: The silhouette is dominated by a structured, ultra-cropped burgundy jacket made of a smooth, matte fabric. It features long, slim sleeves, a wide sailor-style collar trimmed with broad white lace, and an open front. Layered underneath is a tight-fitting, structured corset top in dark green with thin, dense vertical pinstripes. It has a straight neckline accentuated by delicate white lace and a small red center bow, a vertical column of small gold decorative buttons down the front, and a pointed, dip-waist hemline. A high-waisted, flared pleated mini skirt matches the dark green pinstripe fabric of the corset. It features a flat waistband with a small rectangular metallic buckle and a hemline trimmed in wide, scalloped white lace. A thin, long red string tie loops around the neck and drapes down the front. All footwear and knee-high socks are completely removed, leaving the shins, calves, and feet entirely bare.
