This elegant, retro-inspired outfit is dominated by solid white, contrasted by black accessories. The centerpiece is a lightweight, flowing white midi dress with a fitted, structured bodice, a sweetheart neckline, and gathered detailing across the bust. It features off-the-shoulder straps that tie into soft bows on the upper arms, leaving the neck, shoulders, and collarbones exposed. The dress cinches at the natural waist before flowing into an A-line skirt with a high, thigh-high front slit. Accessories include a small structured black leather handbag with a gold clasp, and minimalist black stiletto sandals with thin ankle and toe straps.
