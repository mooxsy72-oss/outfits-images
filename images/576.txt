This casual, layered outfit relies entirely on a dark, muted palette, with a dominant solid matte black upper body contrasted by a secondary medium-grey lower half. The top layer is a structured, heavyweight wool blouson jacket. It features an oversized, boxy silhouette that is cropped at the natural waist. The jacket is constructed with an upright stand collar, wide long sleeves, thick rib-knit cuffs, and a matching rib-knit hem. A vertical snap-button placket closes the front, while a horizontal zippered welt pocket details the left chest and leather-trimmed slash pockets sit at the waist. Beneath it sits a secondary, lightweight knit button-up cardigan. It features a high, upright mock neckline, a standard fit, and a textured, ribbed vertical knit pattern in charcoal grey. The lower body consists of relaxed, wide-leg workwear trousers crafted from medium-weight corduroy fabric. They have a straight-cut silhouette and feature large, rounded top-entry patch pockets extending down the thighs. Accessories include fitted leather gloves crafted from smooth black leather with three top-stitched dart lines running down the back of each hand. The outfit provides full body coverage, leaving no skin exposed.
