OUTFIT: This relaxed home ensemble is built around a loose ivory cotton T-shirt with slightly oversized proportions, dropped sleeves, and a soft washed texture, tucked loosely into high-waisted dusty-blue jersey trousers. The trousers feature an exceptionally soft brushed surface, wide legs, a drawstring waistband, and deep side pockets. Over the shoulders is a chunky rust-orange cardigan made from fluffy boucle yarn, with large buttons and a slightly oversized fit. Thick cream socks and simple knitted slippers complete the outfit. The combination of faded colors, tactile fabrics, and relaxed proportions gives it an authentic lazy-Sunday-at-home feeling.
