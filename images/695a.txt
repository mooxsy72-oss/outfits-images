This bi-color, casual yet elegant ensemble divides focus between a voluminous light gray top and a sleek, elongated black lower half. The upper garment is a heavy-weight, ribbed-knit cropped sweater featuring a thick turtleneck collar and a loose, boxy fit. Its extra-long, wide sleeves feature prominent turned-back cuffs that extend over the hands, while the high-cropped hem ends right under the bust. The lower garment is a high-waisted, floor-length maxi skirt made from a smooth, matte, fluidly draping woven fabric. It features a fitted column silhouette that grips the waist, hips, and upper thighs before cascading down with gentle shape retention to puddle slightly over the feet, leaving no skin exposed. Hidden beneath the hem are sleek black leather boots with a rounded toe box and a flat sole.
