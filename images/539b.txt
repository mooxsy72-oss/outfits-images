This feminine contemporary outfit features a fitted dark brown top, high-waisted beige pleated shorts, and a thin brown belt. The long-sleeved bodysuit top is made from a thin, form-fitting knit fabric in deep chocolate brown, featuring a deep V-neckline trimmed with delicate, sheer black floral lace that extends onto the cuffs. It is tucked cleanly into high-waisted, flared beige shorts crafted from a mid-weight woven fabric with stiff drape. The shorts have sharp front box pleats that flare outward, creating a full, skirt-like silhouette ending at the upper thigh, exposing the thighs, knees, and lower legs. A thin, matching brown leather belt with a silver clasp cinches the waist. All outerwear, footwear, and socks are completely removed, leaving the feet entirely bare.
