The outfit centers on a smooth, fine-gauge black knit turtleneck sweater providing complete upper body and neck coverage. This is tucked into high-waisted, straight-leg tailored black trousers in a heavy wool blend or structured canvas, which offer a clean, formal silhouette without being too tight or overly voluminous. A slim black leather belt with a polished square silver buckle cinches the waist. The color palette is strictly monochromatic black. On the feet, polished black leather boots anchor the refined, balanced proportions of the lower half.
