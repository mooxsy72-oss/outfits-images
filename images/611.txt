This romantic, ethereal ensemble features a uniform soft white color palette defined entirely by a delicate, all-over floral lace pattern. The single garment is an ultra-short mini dress blending a structured corset-style bodice with whimsical, flowing elements. The bodice is tightly fitted to the torso with vertical structured seams, a straight-across sweetheart neckline, and a thin drawstring tie at the center bust, leaving the collarbones and neck exposed. The long sleeves are slim through the upper arms before dramatically flaring out into wide, voluminous bell cuffs at the forearms. Below the waist, the fabric flows into an airy, gathered flared skirt with a ruffled hem that leaves the thighs completely bare. On the feet are pointed-toe stiletto pumps embellished with a prominent, glittering rhinestone bow on the toe box.
