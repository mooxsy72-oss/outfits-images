This casual, high-contrast outfit features a fitted white mini dress paired with a cropped black denim jacket. The sleeveless dress has a form-fitting silhouette, thin camisole straps, and a sweetheart neckline, exposing the shoulders, collarbones, and most of the legs. The fabric is a lightweight, textured knit with a vertical pointelle or ribbed pattern. Layered over it is a heavy, oversized black denim jacket with an ultra-cropped hemline, a standard collar, and long, slightly voluminous sleeves. The jacket features prominent white contrast stitching detailing the seams, pockets, and front panels. The footwear consists of white platform high-top canvas sneakers with black stripe details on the thick rubber midsoles. The entire outfit relies on a solid black-and-white color scheme.
