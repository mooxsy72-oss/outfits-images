OUTFIT: This Y2K-inspired casual ensemble coordinates an off-the-shoulder cream top with ultra-low-rise denim shorts. The fitted, asymmetric rib-knit top features a flexible, medium-weight texture that clings to the torso before flaring into a split, lettuce-edge hemline. It features an elasticated off-the-shoulder neckline exposing the collarbones and shoulders, long sleeves that flare into wide bell cuffs, and a decorative, oversized satin bow pinned near the collarbone. The dark-wash grey denim micro-shorts feature an extreme low-rise waistband with a button closure, leaving the entire midriff, upper hip area, and thighs fully exposed. Draped from the right belt loop is a prominent black star-shaped chain charm. All platform footwear and slouchy striped leg warmers are completely removed, leaving the legs and feet entirely bare. Layered silver chains and a black choker necklace complete the accessories.
