This striking, high-contrast ensemble incorporates Coquette and Gothic Lolita elements through its romantic textures and hyper-feminine proportions.The centerpiece is a close-fitting black satin slip mini dress featuring a V-neckline with a delicate center bow and a flared, ruffled lace hem that terminates at the upper thighs. Layered above is a white lace-trimmed bolero shrug crafted from lightweight, sheer chiffon. This cropped garment features a high mandarin collar with small black buttons, a keyhole chest opening, and voluminous balloon sleeves that gather deeply into elongated cuffs adorned with ruffled lace trims and matching buttons. Sheer black tights cover the legs, leading down to structured, high-gloss black patent leather ankle boots with thick platform soles and block heels. The outfit relies entirely on a solid, unpatterned black-and-white color scheme.
