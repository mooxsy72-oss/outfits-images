OUTFIT: This practical autumn dacha ensemble uses deep natural colors and substantial fabrics. The dominant garment is a muted moss-green field jacket with a relaxed mid-thigh silhouette, broad shoulders, high collar, four large utility pockets, and durable brushed-cotton construction. Beneath it is a chunky dark-brown wool sweater with a loose fit and ribbed cuffs. The lower body consists of straight charcoal canvas trousers with reinforced knees and slightly stacked ankles. Black-brown leather work boots with thick soles provide a rugged foundation. Brown leather gloves and a simple canvas waist pouch complete the outfit.
