This all-black, monochromatic ensemble features three contemporary garments. The dominant volume comes from high-waisted, extra-wide-leg trousers crafted from a structured, mid-weight woven fabric with prominent front pleats that hold their shape down to the floor. Serving as a sharp textural and proportional contrast, the secondary element is a slim-fitting, long-sleeved knit top with an asymmetrical, off-the-shoulder neckline, fine ruched draping across the torso, and an elongated, pointed handkerchief hemline that leaves the lower hip area partially exposed. The footwear consists of minimal, high-gloss patent leather stiletto sandals featuring slender ankle straps and thin toe bands, grounding the outfit with high visual contrast.
