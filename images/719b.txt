This minimalist lounge ensemble focuses entirely on a soft sandy-beige and white color palette, shifting to a sleek, form-fitting silhouette. The upper outerwear is removed, exposing a skin-tight sandy-beige jumpsuit that smoothly covers the entire torso, long arms, and legs in a seamless, body-con fit. Thick-soled, platform white snow boots remain the only voluminous element, detailed with criss-cross laces and a plush white faux-fur trim along the top collar, providing a heavy visual anchor to the slim outfit.
