This dark, monochromatic ensemble balances sleek, body-contouring lines with an exaggeratedly voluminous outer layer in charcoal gray and black tones. The foundational piece is a form-fitting, ankle-length maxi dress made of a medium-weight, highly elastic knit fabric with high shape retention. It features a simple round scoop neckline, a seamless waist construction, and a tight column silhouette that hugs the torso, hips, and legs, exposing only the neck and collarbones. Layered over it is a dramatically oversized, cropped bomber-style jacket crafted from thick, structured black faux-leather. The jacket dominates the silhouette with extreme volume, featuring dropped shoulders, wide balloon sleeves with buckled wrist straps, a broad pointed collar, and a metal zipper front. Finishing the look are pointed-toe black leather ankle boots with high stiletto heels. The sole accessory is a small, structured cylindrical barrel bag held by a short top handle.
