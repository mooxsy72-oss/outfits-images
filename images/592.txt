This minimalist summer loungewear outfit consists of lightweight lower layers, leaving the entire torso, shoulders, and arms completely bare. The dominant color is solid white, with a secondary palette of light blue and white stripes on the shorts, and a small black accent on the socks.The single waist garment features relaxed boxer-style pajama shorts made from a thin, crisp woven cotton fabric. They are constructed with a gathered elastic waistband that sits low on the hips and are patterned entirely with a small-scale, vertical light blue and white stripe. The legs are otherwise bare down to the mid-calf, where thick white crew socks featuring a small black logo on the outer ribbing cover the lower legs and feet entirely.
