This Y2K-inspired casual outfit features a solid, tri-color palette of white and light blue denim without patterns. The secondary upper piece is a tight, cropped white tube top made of smooth, stretchy jersey knit that fully exposes the shoulders, collarbones, and midriff. The dominant lower piece is an ultra-short denim micro-mini skirt constructed from thick, stiff light-wash blue cotton denim. The skirt's structure relies on an intentionally extreme, exaggerated design featuring two oversized, crisscrossing belts with prominent, rectangular metallic buckles that wrap around the low hips, creating asymmetric geometric volume. The silver metallic sandals are completely removed, leaving the legs and feet entirely bare.
