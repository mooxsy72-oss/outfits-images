The outfit centers on a mid-thigh length cable-knit sweater dress in a rich cinnamon-brown tone. It is constructed from a heavy, thick yarn featuring prominent vertical ribbing and a centered vertical cable-knit pattern running down the torso and sleeves. The dress features a tall, structured fold-over turtleneck collar and long, slouchy bishop sleeves that bunch loosely at the ribbed cuffs. The fit is slightly relaxed yet contours to the body, ending with a ribbed hem. Layered underneath are ultra-sheer black nylon tights that offer light coverage down the legs. The color distribution is entirely solid, dominated by cinnamon brown with black as a deep secondary accent. The knee-high boots are completely removed, leaving the legs covered only in the sheer black tights.
