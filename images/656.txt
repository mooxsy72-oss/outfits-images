This monochromatic outfit is entirely black, contrasting structured tailoring with sheer and lace elements. The foundational layer is a tight, micro-length mini dress featuring an asymmetrical, one-shoulder neckline crafted from sheer floral lace, with an angled lace-trimmed hem that exposes the upper thighs. Layered over it is a structured, mid-weight blazer with an oversized, boxy silhouette, padded shoulders, notched lapels, and long straight sleeves. A thin black leather belt with a gold buckle cinches the blazer sharply at the waist, anchoring the shape. The look is styled with ultra-sheer black tights covering the legs, black pointed-toe stiletto pumps, small gold hoop earrings, and a petite black leather handbag with a gold chain strap.
