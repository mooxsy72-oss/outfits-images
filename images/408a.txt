OUTFIT: This modern Japanese "kawaii" and "jirai kei" influenced outfit features a dominant pastel pink and soft white palette. The primary piece is a structured white cotton button-up shirt with a pointed collar, accented by a contrasting satin-textured pink bow tie. This is paired with a short, pleated pink skirt. The lower body features full skin coverage, beginning with sheer off-white tights adorned with a vertical open-work diamond lattice pattern down the side, punctuated by tiny pink ribbon bows. Layered over the tights are exaggeratedly thick, slouchy white knit leg warmers that puddle over the footwear. The shoes are pink platform mary janes with thick white rubber soles and a decorative white ruffle trim running across the wide instep straps.
