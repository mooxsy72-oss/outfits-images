This semi-formal smart-casual outfit has a grayscale color palette dominated by charcoal gray and medium gray, accented by a neutral plaid pattern.
The outer layer is a cropped, boxy charcoal gray bomber jacket made of thick, structured felted wool. It features an exaggerated, voluminous silhouette with a classic turn-down shirt collar, dropped shoulders, long slouchy sleeves finishing in ribbed cuffs, and a wide elasticated waistband. Two prominent patch pockets with rectangular flaps sit on the chest alongside a central silver-toned zipper closure.
Underneath, a stiff white dress shirt with a crisp pointed collar is fully buttoned, layered with a medium-scale plaid necktie in shades of tan, cream, and olive.
The bottom half consists of high-waisted, oversized wide-leg trousers in a medium gray fabric with a subtle vertical pinstripe pattern. The trousers feature deep front pleats that add significant volume, flowing downward to cover the lower half of the body.
