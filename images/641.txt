The outfit features a fluid, tailored office aesthetic combining a silk-like taupe shirt and voluminous dark grey trousers. The solid taupe shirt is constructed from a lightweight, fluid fabric that drapes softly over the upper body. It has a loose silhouette with full-length sleeves, a pointed collar, and an unbuttoned V-neckline that exposes the collarbones. Tucked tightly at the natural waist, it meets the visually dominant dark grey wide-leg trousers. Made from a mid-weight, structured woven suiting fabric with sharp shape retention, the trousers feature a high-rise waist with belt loops, prominent pressed center creases down each leg, and full-length hems that drape over the shoes. A thin, smooth charcoal grey leather belt with a polished silver-tone square buckle cinches the waist. Peeking from under the trouser hems are dark grey snakeskin-embossed pointed-toe pumps. A large, structured taupe leather tote bag with top handles finishes the look.
