OUTFIT: This relaxed home outfit combines classic flannel with soft cotton basics. The dominant piece is an oversized red-and-forest-green plaid flannel shirt with a brushed fuzzy surface, dropped shoulders, long loose sleeves, and large chest pockets, worn open over a simple fitted cream cotton camisole. The lower body consists of loose charcoal-grey jersey shorts reaching mid-thigh, featuring a comfortable drawstring waist and slightly rolled hems. Thick dark socks and simple leather-like house slippers complete the look. The contrast between structured plaid and soft casual jersey gives the outfit an effortlessly lived-in weekend feeling.
