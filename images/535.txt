OUTFIT: This casual home look centers on an oversized heather-grey fleece hoodie dress with an extremely soft brushed interior, dropped shoulders, a large hood, long sleeves, and a loose hem ending several inches above the knees. The silhouette is intentionally oversized without appearing shapeless, with ribbed cuffs keeping the sleeves neatly gathered around the wrists. Beneath it are opaque black ribbed knee-high socks and chunky cream-colored knitted house slippers. A small fabric hair tie around the wrist adds a lived-in detail. The monochromatic grey-and-cream palette keeps the oversized proportions clean and effortless.
