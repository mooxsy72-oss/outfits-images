This monochrome, sporty ensemble is dominated by a uniform dark charcoal grey, lacking any intricate patterns or embroidery. The primary garment is a form-fitting, short-style romper or playsuit crafted from a lightweight, highly elastic ribbed knit fabric. It contours tightly to the torso and hips, ending in very short leg openings that leave the thighs fully exposed. The top section features extra-long, tight sleeves that extend past the wrists to cover part of the hands. A central button placket runs down the front, which is left unbuttoned to form a deep, plunging V-neckline that exposes the chest. Accessories include a matching dark grey baseball cap with a structured crown, a curved brim, and a small, white embroidered abstract logo on the front. A minimal, delicate gold chain necklace with a tiny dark clover pendant rests on the collarbone.
