This two-piece ensemble combines a structured cream corset-style top with a fluid, tiered mini skirt in a muted sage green and gray palette. The fitted top features thick shoulder straps, a square neckline, and visible vertical boning seams that give the medium-weight fabric shape retention, tapering to a curved hem that reveals a sliver of midriff. The lightweight, low-rise mini skirt has a fluid drape and a repeating small-scale floral pattern, utilizing two ruffled layers to create visual volume at the upper thighs. The footwear is completely removed, leaving the feet entirely bare.
