This modern luxury loungewear set features a relaxed, layered pajama silhouette. The dominant color is a solid pale sage green or light olive-grey across all primary garments, with a secondary accent of solid white at the waist.The outerwear layer consists of a knee-length waffle-knit robe with loose, slightly oversized proportions. It features a collarless open-front design with a wide shawl-style band, dropped shoulders, relaxed long sleeves, and a matching fabric tie-belt hanging loosely at the waist. Underneath, the matching waffle-knit pajama trousers feature a straight, wide-leg cut that drapes softly to the feet. They are constructed with a gathered elastic waistband revealing a white inner lining, a two-button faux fly detail at the front, and side slip pockets. The torso and chest are left completely exposed.
