This relaxed Y2K-inspired all-denim outfit focuses on a casual, indoor look. The top is a highly structured, mid-weight blue denim halter with a plunging V-neckline, pointed handkerchief hem, and a central gold zip. It exposes the shoulders, arms, and full midriff. The low-rise, slim-fit jeans feature a subtle flare at the hem, classic five-pocket construction, and light fading down the thighs. The heels are completely removed, leaving the feet entirely bare. A delicate gold pendant necklace and thin gold bracelets remain as the only accessories.
