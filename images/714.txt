The outfit centers on an exaggerated, ankle-length double-breasted overcoat in a thick, heavyweight matte black wool cloth. It features strongly padded, structured shoulders, wide peaked lapels, long straight sleeves, and a deep front button layout that produces a dramatically elongated, flared silhouette. Layered beneath is a medium-weight black knit half-zip sweater with a tall, structured funnel-neck collar and a prominent metallic silver zipper down the chest. This is tucked into high-waisted, straight-leg tailored trousers in solid black fabric that offer a clean, formal silhouette without being too tight or overly voluminous. A slim black leather belt with a subtle buckle cinches the high waist. The entire color distribution is strictly monochromatic black with no visible patterns. On the feet, square-toed black leather loafers anchor the refined, structured drape of the trousers.
