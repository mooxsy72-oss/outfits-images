This feminine contemporary outfit features a fitted dark brown top, high-waisted beige pleated shorts, a thin brown belt, white frilled socks, and tall dark brown boots. The long-sleeved bodysuit top is made from a thin, form-fitting knit fabric in deep chocolate brown, featuring a deep V-neckline trimmed with delicate, sheer black floral lace that extends onto the cuffs. It is tucked cleanly into high-waisted, flared beige shorts crafted from a mid-weight woven fabric with stiff drape. The shorts have sharp front box pleats that flare outward, creating a full, skirt-like silhouette ending at the upper thigh, exposing the lower thighs and knees. A thin, matching brown leather belt with a silver clasp cinches the waist. The look is grounded by tall, structured dark brown leather riding boots that rise to just below the knee, with white socks featuring delicate frilled lace trim emerging from the top of the boots.
