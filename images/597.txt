This minimalist, modern post-bath loungewear look features a single draped element, leaving the entire upper torso, chest, back, shoulders, and arms completely exposed. The dominant color is solid white with no secondary tones or patterns.The single item consists of a large rectangular bath towel used as a waist wrap. It is crafted from a thick, heavy-weight, absorbent cotton terry-cloth fabric with a dense, looped texture that retains a structured, bulky volume. Wrapped tightly around the natural waist, it is secured at the center-front by twisting and tucking the top corners into a thick, rolled knot. The fabric drapes straight down into a column-like silhouette, covering the hips, thighs, and upper legs before terminating just below the knees.
