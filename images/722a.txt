This minimalist outfit features a clean cream, beige, and black color palette without any handheld accessories. The upper layer consists of a slouchy, cropped beige knit sweater crafted from a soft, medium-weight melange yarn, featuring a ribbed mock neck and relaxed balloon sleeves with elongated ribbed cuffs. The lower body is defined by high-waisted, secondary cream wide-leg denim jeans that provide a heavy, structured drape down to the floor. Completing the look is a pair of sleek cream leather ankle boots with chunky block heels and softly squared pointed toes.
