This military-inspired streetwear outfit features a balanced color distribution dominated by neutral medium gray and matte black. The primary upper piece is a thick, relaxed-fit medium gray fleece pullover hoodie with a substantial double-layered hood framing the neck, long sleeves finishing in ribbed cuffs, and a kangaroo pocket. The bottom half consists of slim-fit black tactical cargo pants made of structured, lightweight cotton ripstop, featuring prominent rectangular utility pockets on the outer thighs. On the feet are classic high-top canvas sneakers in solid black with contrasting stark white rubber toe caps, white laces, and white soles.
