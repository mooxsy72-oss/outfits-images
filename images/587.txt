OUTFIT: This formal autumn look uses an almost-black burgundy-brown leather jacket as its distinctive element. The jacket is waist-length with a clean minimalist cut, broad shoulders, a straight hem, and exceptionally smooth matte leather. Beneath it is a deep brown merino turtleneck tucked into dark chocolate wool trousers with a relaxed straight cut and pressed crease. The footwear consists of polished dark brown leather monk-strap shoes. The burgundy undertone remains subtle, visible mainly where the leather catches the light. COLOR PALETTE: black-burgundy, chocolate, dark brown.
