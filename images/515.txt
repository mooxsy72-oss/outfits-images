OUTFIT: This refined athletic ensemble reinterprets modern golfwear through a fitted silhouette. The dominant garment is a sleeveless ivory polo bodysuit with a structured collar, concealed buttons, close waist, and smooth stretch fabric. A short pleated charcoal tennis-golf skirt sits high on the waist, featuring built-in compression shorts and sharply defined knife pleats. Over the shoulders is a fitted cropped burgundy knit cardigan with small buttons and ribbed edges. White leather golf sneakers, ankle socks, and minimal silver jewelry complete the polished sporty aesthetic.
