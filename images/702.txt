The outfit centers on a long, oversized double-breasted wrap overcoat in a thick, heavyweight matte beige wool blend. It features wide, unstructured notched lapels, significantly dropped shoulders, broad straight sleeves with decorative wrist tabs, and deep patch pockets, creating an enveloping, voluminous silhouette. Layered beneath is a cropped, medium-weight cream knit turtleneck sweater with an exaggerated ribbed fold-over collar. The sweater features a dramatic stepped high-low hemline with split open sides, exposing the high waistline. This is paired with high-waisted, slim-fit tailored trousers in a matching soft beige shade, showcasing a smooth front panel and sharp center creases. The overall palette is entirely monochromatic, utilizing tones of beige and cream. To complement this sophisticated sartorial look, add a pair of polished cream leather pointed-toe heeled ankle boots to elevate the long, fluid proportions.
