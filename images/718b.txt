This minimalist lounge ensemble focuses entirely on a soft white and cream color palette with chestnut accents. The silhouette is defined by comfortable, relaxed proportions and soft fabrics. The upper body features a thick, soft white long-sleeve knit sweater that is tucked into high-waisted, wide-leg cream sweatpants with a drawstring waistband. The trousers fall in a loose, straight silhouette that drapes heavily down to the feet. Thick-soled, platform chestnut suede Ugg-style shearling boots add a single contrasting element of secondary color and anchor the relaxed, comfortable lower body silhouette.
