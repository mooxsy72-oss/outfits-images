This monochromatic, deep chocolate brown ensemble balances contrasting textures by pairing a heavy ribbed knit with a smooth, structured woven fabric. The upper layer is an oversized, cropped sweater featuring a wide vertical rib-knit texture and a shallow V-neckline. Its dropped shoulders lead into dramatic, voluminous balloon sleeves that gather at the wrists with thick cuffs, while the straight, high-cropped hem completely exposes the midriff. In contrast, the tight-fitting bodycon mini-skirt is constructed from a smooth, matte, non-textured midweight fabric. It features a prominent, overlapping asymmetrical waistband formed by two wide, flat panels that cross over each other to create a clean V-shape just below the navel, covering the hips and exposing the thighs. Finishing the silhouette, high knee-length leather boots in matching dark brown feature a matte finish, wide straight shafts, rounded toes, and flat soles, leaving the knees fully visible.
