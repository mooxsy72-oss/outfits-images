This monochrome ensemble relies completely on a solid matte black texture, creating a uniform, dark color palette. An underlying matte black knit dress serves as the main piece, featuring a high, tight turtleneck collar and a very short hemline, cinched at the natural waist by a narrow black leather belt with a small silver buckle. The legs and feet are fully covered by the smooth, sheer black hosiery, providing continuous coverage down to the toes, with no shoes worn. The look is accented only by small, rounded silver button earrings.
