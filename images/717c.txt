This indoor lounge ensemble focuses entirely on a soft cream and beige color palette, maintaining comfortable, slouchy proportions and soft textures. The upper body features a thick cream knit turtleneck sweater with vertical ribbed textures, a high folded collar, and relaxed long sleeves that drape over the wrists. This is tucked into high-waisted, voluminous cream sweatpants with a drawstring waistband. The trousers feature heavy, gathered fabric that creates fullness through the legs before tapering tightly into ribbed ankles. The feet are bare of footwear, covered only in thick, ribbed cream socks.
