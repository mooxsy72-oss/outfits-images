The outfit features a tailored monochrome aesthetic in a solid taupe-brown hue, pairing a tight base layer with an ankle-length outerwear layer. The visually dominant ankle-length overcoat is made from a heavy, structured wool-like fabric with strong shape retention, featuring padded shoulders, notched lapels, and long straight sleeves draped over the shoulders. Layered gold chain necklaces, gold hoop earrings, and a gold watch complete the look.
