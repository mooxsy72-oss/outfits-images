This outfit centers around a pale yellow maxi dress paired with minimal heels. The dress features a highly structured, corset-style bodice with defined underwire cups, visible vertical boning lines, and ultra-thin spaghetti straps, leaving the chest, collarbones, and shoulders bare. Constructed with a defined natural waistline, it flows into a voluminous, gathered maxi skirt. The skirt is made of a lightweight, translucent chiffon-like fabric that creates dramatic volume, featuring a high front thigh slit that exposes the leg. Accessories include a thin gold pendant necklace, a slim gold bracelet, and neutral mule sandals with a transparent strap.
