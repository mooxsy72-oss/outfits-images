This winter ensemble relies on a dominant cream and off-white palette, accented by secondary pastel baby-blue elements and chestnut footwear. The look features a padded, cropped puffer jacket in light baby blue with horizontal quilting, a high collar, and elasticated cuffs that add rounded volume to the upper torso. Beneath, wide-leg cream sweatpants feature a high-rise drawstring waist, falling in a relaxed, straight silhouette that drapes heavily over the shoes. A thick, off-white knit scarf with long fringed ends wraps around the neck, hanging down the front. A simple, ribbed baby-blue beanie covers the head, while cream knit mittens cover the hands. Thick-soled, platform chestnut suede Ugg-style shearling boots anchor the outfit, providing full body coverage.
