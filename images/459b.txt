OUTFIT: This elegant, high-contrast evening ensemble features a solid color palette of dominant champagne-cream and secondary deep burgundy, devoid of patterns. The silhouette is streamlined and body-skimming, exposing the chest, collarbones, and the entire left leg from the mid-thigh down. The upper garment is a tight, form-fitting bodysuit with long, slim-fit sleeves and a wide, geometric square neckline. It is crafted from a lightweight, matte burgundy jersey knit that possesses high stretch and low shape retention. The lower garment is a high-waisted, floor-length wrap skirt in a champagne-cream tone, featuring a wide fabric waistband that secures into a long, fluid side tie. The skirt drapes down with an extreme, thigh-high side slit. Its fabric is a medium-weight satin with a high-gloss sheen and a liquid-like drape that falls under gravity. The footwear is completely removed, leaving the feet entirely bare.
