This summer-casual ensemble features a dominant palette of solid white and light-blue denim with neutral accessories. The upper body wears a fitted, cropped corset-style tank top in thick white knit fabric with a square neckline, wide shoulder straps, and prominent vertical boning seams that shape a curved, pointed hemline, exposing the arms, neck, and a sliver of midriff. This is paired with high-waisted, short denim shorts in a light wash with classic five-pocket styling, a button closure, and heavily frayed, raw-cut leg openings. The athletic sneakers are completely removed; the feet are covered only in mid-calf white crew socks. Accessories include a delicate layered gold chain necklace with a small square pendant and a simple gold chain bracelet.
