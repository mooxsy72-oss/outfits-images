This minimalist, modern undergarment and lounge look features tightly fitted lower layers, leaving the entire torso, chest, back, shoulders, arms, and thighs completely exposed. The dominant colors are solid black and solid white in a clean, non-patterned color distribution.The primary garment consists of form-fitting trunks or boxer briefs made from a thin, elastic cotton-blend knit fabric that tightly retains its shape against the skin. They feature a low-rise fit sitting on the hips with a thick, contrasting white elastic waistband adorned with thin black text branding. The hemline cuts high across the upper thighs. The lower legs feature thick white crew socks in a ribbed cotton knit that cover the feet and pull up tightly just above the ankles. Subtle accessories include a thin silver pendant necklace and a matching silver chain bracelet.
