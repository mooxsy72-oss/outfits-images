OUTFIT: This layered, textured ensemble centers on a rich, deep burgundy color palette, varying only in texture, opacity, and material weight. The primary full-body garment is a fitted mini dress crafted from an ultra-lightweight, sheer burgundy mesh or fine-knit fabric. The dress features a high, draped turtleneck collar and long, slim sleeves. It hugs the torso and waist closely, ending in a straight, sheer hemline at the mid-thigh to offer a high degree of leg exposure. The footwear consists of knee-high boots in a matching dark burgundy hue. They feature a structured, smooth leather or suede shaft that fits cleanly over the calves, a sharply pointed toe, and a high stiletto heel.
