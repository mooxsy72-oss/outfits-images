The outfit features a balanced smart-casual aesthetic composed of a deep burgundy bodysuit, high-waisted grey trousers, and a coordinating grey cropped cardigan. The solid deep burgundy bodysuit is tightly form-fitting, featuring a plunging sweetheart neckline and thin spaghetti straps that leave the shoulders, collarbones, and upper chest exposed. Tucked smoothly under the trousers, it sits beneath the visually dominant grey wide-leg trousers. Made from a mid-weight woven suiting fabric with sharp shape retention, these trousers feature a high-rise waist with belt loops and full-length legs that drape to the floor. A thin black leather belt with a small, polished silver-tone square buckle cinches the waist. Draped loosely off the shoulders is a matching grey knit cardigan with oversized, slouchy sleeves. On the feet are black leather pointed-toe pumps.
