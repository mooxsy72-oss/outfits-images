This outfit is a formal three-piece tailored suit in a monochromatic charcoal gray palette, broken only by dark brown accents. The silhouette is structured and classic, dominated by a heavy, matte wool fabric with excellent shape retention and a clean drape.The outermost layer is a double-breasted suit jacket featuring wide peak lapels, structured padded shoulders, full-length sleeves, and prominent black buttons. Beneath it sits a matching tailored waistcoat with a V-neckline, a pointed hem, a neat row of central buttons, and a double-strand antique gold pocket watch chain draped across the lower left side.Under the waistcoat is a dark brown dress shirt with a pointed collar, paired with a matching dark brown silk tie secured by a simple silver tie clip. The high-waisted trousers match the jacket and waistcoat, featuring a relaxed straight-leg fit, distinct front pleats, sharp center creases, and structured cuffed hems that rest neatly on dark brown leather Oxford dress shoes. The entire ensemble provides full body coverage from the neck down.
