This modern athletic training outfit focuses on a close-fitting, streamlined silhouette optimized for movement. The dominant color is solid black on the upper body, paired with a secondary space-dye or heathered grey pattern on the shorts, and solid white accents at the feet.The upper garment is a fitted black tank top (A-shirt) made from a lightweight, ribbed cotton-spandex blend that clings to the torso, featuring deep sleeveless armholes and a scooped neckline that leaves the arms and neck fully exposed. The lower garment consists of lightweight athletic running shorts crafted from a thin, fluid performance synthetic fabric, sitting high on the waist and terminating high on the thighs. The shorts display a dense, fine-scale horizontal heathered grey pattern. The lower legs feature thick white crew socks pulled tightly above the ankles, leaving the feet covered but shoes absent.
