This two-piece loungewear set in a solid, muted sage green consists of a camisole top and matching shorts made from a lightweight, soft, fluid jersey knit with a gentle drape and high stretch. The slim-fit camisole features thin spaghetti straps, a low scooped neckline with small ruffled trim, and a dainty fabric tie at the center chest, leaving the shoulders, collarbones, and arms completely bare. The high-waisted shorts have a gathered elastic waistband with an adjustable drawstring and a relaxed, flared leg that finishes mid-thigh. Flouncy, gathered ruffle trim edges the curved leg openings, adding soft volume to the lower half. The monochrome outfit exposes the shoulders, arms, lower midriff, and most of the legs, forming a soft, relaxed silhouette.
