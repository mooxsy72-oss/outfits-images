This summer-inspired ensemble features a floral corset-style top paired with high-waisted, wide-leg trousers. The top is a structured, strapless sweetheart bustier that tapers into a pointed handkerchief hem. It is made of a smooth, lightweight fabric featuring a prominent white background with vibrant pink, yellow, and green floral patterns and a green border trim, leaving the shoulders, arms, and collarbones exposed. The high-waisted, off-white linen trousers feature a voluminous, relaxed silhouette with front pleats and a floor-length drape that dominates the lower half. Accessories include a delicate layered gold pendant necklace, a gold bracelet, and open-toe block-heeled sandals.
