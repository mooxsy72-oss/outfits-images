This casual summer outfit pairs a textured yellow top with light-wash denim shorts and white accessories. The top is a slim-fit, long-sleeved shirt made from semi-sheer, pale yellow floral lace with a wide boat neckline and dramatic, flared bell sleeves. It covers the upper body but reveals the collarbones. The low-rise denim shorts feature a distressed, frayed hemline and a classic five-pocket design, exposing the entire length of the legs. A small, structured off-white crescent shoulder bag hangs neatly under the arm. The footwear consists of white strappy sandals featuring a square toe and slender laces that wrap tightly around the ankles.
