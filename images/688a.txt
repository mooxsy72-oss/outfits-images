This chic, minimalist summer ensemble features a structured dark brown mini dress. The sleeveless mini dress is crafted from a rigid, heavy woven fabric that firmly retains its shape. It features a fitted bodice with tailored vertical princess seams, a clean square neckline, and thick halter-style shoulder straps that expose the collarbones, shoulders, and arms. The high waistline transitions into a structured, flared A-line skirt that holds its shape away from the body, ending mid-thigh. The overall palette is entirely unpatterned solid earth tones. The slingback pumps are completely removed, leaving the feet entirely bare.
