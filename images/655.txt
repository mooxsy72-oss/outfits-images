This modern three-piece ensemble features a solid black color palette accented by a single white underlayer. The cropped black jacket is structured with padded, wide shoulders, long straight sleeves, and a classic notched lapel, hanging open to reveal a fitted white crop top underneath that exposes the midriff. The matching black midi skirt is high-waisted, tightly tailored through the hips with asymmetric gathering or ruching at the side hip, and features a high thigh-length slit that heavily exposes the left leg. Both outer pieces are crafted from a mid-weight, smooth suiting fabric. The outfit is completed with a delicate gold chain necklace, a small matte black leather handbag with a thin shoulder strap, and black strappy stiletto sandals with thin laces wrapping around the ankles.
