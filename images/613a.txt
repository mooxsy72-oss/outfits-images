This sleek, modern resort-style ensemble features a uniform teal blue color palette with a secondary touch of pearly white, lacking any prints or patterns. The outfit is a coordinated two-piece set crafted from a lightweight, smooth jersey or silk-blend fabric that behaves with a heavy, fluid drape and subtle sheen. The top is a multi-way halter wrap bodice that crosses tightly over the chest, forming a keyhole cutout at the upper midriff. The fabric bands narrow as they wrap around the neck, leaving the shoulders, arms, back, and lower midriff fully exposed. This is paired with a high-waisted midi skirt that sits flat on the waist before gathering tightly into a knot detail at the left hip. The gathering creates fluid, liquid-like ripples across the front and gives way to a dramatic, thigh-high asymmetrical leg slit. A single row of small white pearls serves as a choker-style necklace around the neck. The footwear is completely removed, leaving the feet entirely bare.
