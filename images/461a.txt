A modern streetwear ensemble relies on contrasting textures and a high-contrast palette of dominant black and light cream-beige, offering full body coverage without exposed skin. The upper half features an intentionally oversized, voluminous cropped puffer jacket in matte black nylon. It is defined by thick horizontal quilting, a high stand-up padded collar, and bulky sleeves. Layered beneath is a thick, cream-beige sweater with a dense ribbed-knit mock neckline. The lower half consists of ultra-tight, second-skin leggings or trousers in a matching cream-beige tone with a fine vertical ribbed texture. On the feet are a pair of classic, short tan suede Ugg-style boots featuring a plush cream shearling lining, a flat sand-colored sole, and a soft, rounded toe box. The major accessory is a chunky, cream-beige beanie knitted from thick yarn, featuring distinct, pointy cat-ear protrusions at the crown.
