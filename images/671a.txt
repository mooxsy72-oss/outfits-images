This chic, minimalist summer outfit features a tight, ribbed burgundy tank top tucked into high-waisted, tailored white shorts. The tank top has thin shoulder straps and a deep square neckline, leaving the collarbones and arms exposed. The structured white shorts feature tailored front pleats, a clean waistband, and cuffed hems that end mid-thigh. The footwear consists of chunky burgundy leather slide sandals with a thick platform sole. For accessories, a delicate gold chain necklace with a small teardrop-shaped burgundy pendant and round, button-style burgundy stud earrings complete the uniform solid-color aesthetic.
