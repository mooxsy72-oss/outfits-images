OUTFIT: This avant-garde streetwear ensemble features an eclectic, multi-layered color blocking scheme dominated by matte black and heather gray, offering total body coverage. The primary layer consists of a tight, secondary-skin top in thin, high-stretch jersey, tucked into very short, structured heather-gray wool shorts with a curved, high-rise waistband. On the legs are opaque black tights providing full coverage under the garments. Footwear consists of structured, knee-high black leather boots with a smooth matte texture that provides a sleek, polished finish to the lower silhouette.
