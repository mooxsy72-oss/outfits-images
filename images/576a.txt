This casual, layered outfit relies entirely on a dark, muted palette, with a dominant solid matte black upper body contrasted by a secondary medium-grey lower half. The main piece is a lightweight knit button-up cardigan, which is worn completely closed and buttoned up. It features a high, upright mock neckline, a standard fit, and a textured, ribbed vertical knit pattern in charcoal grey. The lower body consists of relaxed, wide-leg workwear trousers crafted from medium-weight corduroy fabric. They have a straight-cut silhouette and feature large, rounded top-entry patch pockets extending down the thighs.
