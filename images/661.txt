A contemporary formal ensemble consists of a cropped black blazer, high-waisted black trousers, rhinestone-accented heels, and silver jewelry. The structured, ultra-cropped blazer features sharp padded shoulders, notched lapels, and long fitted sleeves, ending abruptly below the bust. Suspending from its lower hemline is an intricate corset-like arrangement of draped silver rhinestone chains that exposes the midriff. The heavy, matte black trousers are ultra-high-waisted and wide-legged, incorporating deep front overlap pleats and sharp vertical hem slits that create a voluminous silhouette. Accessories include a rigid silver rhinestone choker with a central teardrop pendant, long dangling earrings shaped like interlocking "C" logos, and black satin pointed-toe stiletto pumps featuring crossing rhinestone ankle straps.
