This modern two-piece shorts suit is monochromatic, dominated by an off-white hue with zero fabric patterns. The cropped jacket is heavily structured with padded, wide shoulders, a notched lapel collar, a single-button closure, and intentionally elongated sleeves that bunch at the wrists. The matching high-waisted shorts are skin-tight and micro-length with clean vertical seams, maximizing leg exposure from the upper thigh down. Both garments are crafted from a thick, stiff woven material that firmly retains its boxy shape. Accessories include a thin gold chain necklace, a small brown monogram canvas shoulder bag with a silver chain, and animal-print open-toe stiletto heels.
