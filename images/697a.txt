This minimalist, casual athleisure outfit utilizes a high-contrast black and light gray color scheme without patterns. The base layer features a tight black crew-neck top. The lower half is a solid black, high-waisted column maxi skirt made of stretchy jersey knit that hugs the hips and falls straight to the ankles. Layered above is a loose, oversized heather gray fleece sweatshirt, neatly cropped to sit right at the natural waistline for a balanced silhouette. It features an open half-zip polo collar, wide dropped shoulders, and long slouchy sleeves that gather with ribbed cuffs. Accessories include a thin silver pendant chain necklace and small gold hoop earrings. The look is completed barefoot.
