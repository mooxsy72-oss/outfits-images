This modern minimalist athletic look features a single waist garment and basic foot coverings, leaving the entire chest, back, shoulders, arms, and midriff completely bare. The dominant color is solid black, with a secondary accent of solid white at the lower legs.The core garment consists of lightweight athletic training shorts made from a thin, fluid performance mesh or synthetic fabric. They feature a flat, elasticized waistband that sits low on the hips and a relaxed, slightly wide-leg cut that terminates high on the mid-thigh. On the lower legs, thick white crew socks crafted from a ribbed cotton blend stretch upward past the ankles to the lower calf. A matte black digital sports watch is worn on the right wrist as the sole accessory.
