This monochromatic dark brown and black outfit features a ribbed knit mini dress, thin double-chain belt, and sheer tights. The dominant piece is a dark chocolate brown, heavy-weight ribbed knit dress featuring a fitted bodice with a high crew neckline. The sleeves are long and slim until the mid-forearm, where they dramatically flare out into wide, pleated bell cuffs. The dress dramatically cinches at the natural waist with an integrated fabric tie, then flares out into a voluminous, micro-mini pleated skater skirt that sits high on the thighs, exposing the legs. Wrapped around the waist is a delicate, decorative silver metal double-chain belt. The lower body is covered by sheer, matte black tights that offer semi-translucent leg coverage. The tall wedge boots are completely removed, leaving the feet and legs covered only in the sheer black tights.
