This monochrome summer look is entirely solid white, lacking any distinct prints, patterns, or embroidery. The single garment is a strapless, ultra-short mini dress blending cottagecore and structured elements.The upper half features a heavily structured, boned corset bodice that tightly cinches the waist and torso. It has a strapless sweetheart neckline with a gathered, ruffled bust section that ties with a delicate, thin fabric bow at the center chest. The entire upper chest, shoulders, back, and arms are left fully exposed. At the natural waist, the structured fabric transitions sharply into a lightweight, airy, and high-volume flared circle skirt. The skirt is heavily gathered at the seam, creating a dramatic, billowy drape that covers the hips but leaves the thighs completely bare.
